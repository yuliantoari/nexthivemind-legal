NEXT Hivemind

Personal analytics and workflow tool for the YouTube channel NEXT Xinema

What it is

NEXT Hivemind is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the channel owner understand the performance of his own YouTube channel (views, watch time, revenue, subscribers), plan content, and, when the owner clicks a button, update the title, description, tags, thumbnail or publish schedule of his own videos.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the channel. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it does not offer sign-up.

Google / YouTube data

The tool connects to the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API with the owner's own Google account. Details of what is accessed and how it is handled are in the Privacy Policy.
